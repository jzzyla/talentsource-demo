Holdout MAE is the average absolute error in quality points across unseen validation wines. R² compares squared error with predicting the validation-set mean; 1 is perfect, and negative values are possible. Neither metric is confidence in this individual estimate.

This is an estimate, not a food-safety or health assessment. The dataset represents a narrow wine population.
